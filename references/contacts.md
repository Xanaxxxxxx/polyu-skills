# Contacts mode

Find the correct lecturer, tutor, department or service and return verified contact details. This is read-only; do not synchronize Notion, send messages, or make calls.

## Course-specific contacts

- For “my Game Design lecturer” or similar requests, establish the user's course and current teaching period from relevant course records. Use an already verified course identity when available; otherwise read only the necessary Notion course record or LMS course list/details using [query](query.md). This limited academic lookup is part of the contact request, not a broad scan.
- Confirm the lecturer/tutor assignment in the current course page, syllabus, teaching-team page, or relevant announcement. Do not equate a public subject coordinator or past instructor with the user's current teacher. If multiple sections have different instructors and the section is unknown, explain the confirmed options and ask only for the missing section.
- Match the confirmed name to the official PolyU staff directory/profile for current email, telephone and office. If the course gives a dedicated contact channel, include that channel and its stated purpose. Do not infer an email address from a name.
- Give name, course/role and relevant email first, then phone/office when available. Link both course evidence and the official contact page. State unavailable office hours or conflicting records rather than inventing availability.
